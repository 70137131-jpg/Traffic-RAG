# City of Neo-Veridia Traffic Regulation Code (2025 Edition)

## Title 1: General Provisions
**Section 101. Definitions**
(a) "Vehicle" means a device by which any person or property may be propelled, moved, or drawn upon a highway, except devices used exclusively upon stationary rails or tracks.
(b) "Autonomous Unit" means any vehicle operating without direct human intervention for a period exceeding 5 minutes.
(c) "Hover-Zone" means areas designated for magnetic levitation transport.

**Section 102. Scope**
These regulations apply to all public roadways, hover-zones, and pedestrian pathways within the municipal limits of Neo-Veridia.

## Title 2: Speed Restrictions
**Section 201. Basic Speed Law**
No person shall drive a vehicle at a speed greater than is reasonable or prudent having due regard for weather, visibility, the traffic on, and the surface and width of, the highway, and in no event at a speed which endangers the safety of persons or property.

**Section 202. Prima Facie Speed Limits**
The following speeds are lawful unless otherwise posted:
(1) 15 miles per hour in school zones when children are present.
(2) 25 miles per hour in any business or residential district.
(3) 55 miles per hour on urban freeways.
(4) 120 kilometers per hour in designated High-Speed Hover Lanes.

**Section 203. Penalties for Excessive Speed**
Exceeding the speed limit by more than 20 mph constitutes "Reckless Velocity" and carries a mandatory fine of 500 Credits and a 30-day suspension of operating privileges.

## Title 3: Right of Way
**Section 301. Intersections**
(a) When two vehicles enter an intersection from different highways at approximately the same time, the driver of the vehicle on the left shall yield the right of way to the vehicle on the right.
(b) Autonomous Units must always yield to human-operated classic vehicles in non-signalized intersections.

**Section 302. Emergency Vehicles**
Upon the immediate approach of an authorized emergency vehicle making use of audible and visual signals, the driver of every other vehicle shall yield the right of way and shall immediately drive to a position parallel to, and as close as possible to, the right-hand edge or curb of the highway clear of any intersection and shall stop and remain in such position until the authorized emergency vehicle has passed.

## Title 4: Parking and Standing
**Section 401. Prohibited Parking**
No person shall stop, park, or leave standing any vehicle whether attended or unattended:
(1) Within an intersection.
(2) On a crosswalk.
(3) Within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.
(4) In a designated "Drone Landing Zone" between the hours of 08:00 and 20:00.

**Section 402. Time Limited Parking**
Green curb markings indicate time limit parking for 20 minutes. Blue curb markings indicate parking limited to vehicles displaying a valid disability placard.

## Title 5: Equipment and Safety
**Section 501. Lighting Equipment**
(a) Every vehicle upon a highway at any time from sunset to sunrise shall display lighted lamps and illuminating devices.
(b) Hover-vehicles must display a downward-facing amber anti-collision light during operation.

**Section 502. Noise Control**
No vehicle shall emit sound exceeding 85 decibels at a distance of 50 feet, except for authorized emergency sirens.

## Title 6: Distracted Driving
**Section 601. Wireless Devices**
(a) A person shall not drive a motor vehicle while holding and operating a handheld wireless telephone or an electronic wireless communications device unless the vehicle is stationary and not located in a part of the public roadway intended for traffic.
(b) Neural-link interfaces must be set to "Driving Mode" (read-only) while the vehicle is in motion.

## Title 7: Cyclists and Pedestrians
**Section 701. Bicycle Lanes**
Motor vehicles may not enter a bicycle lane except to park where permitted, to enter or leave the roadway, or to prepare for a turn within a distance of 200 feet from the intersection.

**Section 702. Pedestrian Right of Way**
The driver of a vehicle shall yield the right-of-way to a pedestrian crossing the roadway within any marked crosswalk or within any unmarked crosswalk at an intersection.
